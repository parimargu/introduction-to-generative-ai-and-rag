Brightpath Technologies — Employee Leave Policy (2026)

1. Purpose

This policy explains the different types of leave available to all full-time employees of Brightpath Technologies, how much leave each employee receives, and the process for applying. It applies to all offices in India, including Chennai, Bengaluru and Pune. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis.

2. Annual (Earned) Leave

Every full-time employee receives 18 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave is credited at the rate of 1.5 days on the first day of each month. Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year; any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 December. Annual leave must be applied for at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal and approved by the reporting manager.

3. Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward and is not encashable. If an employee takes more than 2 consecutive days of sick leave, a medical certificate from a registered doctor must be uploaded to the HR portal within 3 working days of returning. Sick leave can be applied on the same day by informing the manager before 10:00 AM.

4. Casual Leave

Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per year for personal matters such as family functions or urgent errands. Casual leave cannot be taken for more than 3 days in a row and cannot be combined with annual leave. Unused casual leave lapses at the end of the year.

5. Maternity and Paternity Leave

Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children, as per the Maternity Benefit Act. For the third child onwards, maternity leave is 12 weeks. Male employees are entitled to 15 working days of paid paternity leave, which must be used within 3 months of the child's birth. Adoptive parents receive 12 weeks of leave if the child is below 3 months of age.

6. Public Holidays

The company observes 12 public holidays every year. The holiday list for each office is published by HR in December for the following year. Employees who are required to work on a public holiday receive one compensatory off day, which must be used within 60 days.

7. Work From Home

Employees may work from home for up to 8 days per month with manager approval. Work from home is not counted as leave. During work from home, employees must be reachable on Slack between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM IST.

8. Leave Without Pay

If an employee has used all eligible paid leave, they may request leave without pay for a maximum of 30 days in a year. Leave without pay requires approval from both the reporting manager and the HR head. Salary is deducted on a per-day basis for each day of leave without pay.

9. Leave Encashment at Exit

When an employee leaves the company, unused annual leave of up to 30 days is paid out along with the final settlement, calculated on the basic salary. Sick leave and casual leave are never encashed.
